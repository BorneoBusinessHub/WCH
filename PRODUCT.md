# WCH Solutions website

## Product
WCH Solutions Sdn Bhd develops a phased AI data centre in Sarawak, Malaysia, on the state's hydro-led grid. Facts that may be stated: 10 MW power contract, 10-year term, 2 MW Phase 1. Status: power and land secured; detailed design, customer contracts and delivery are next.

## Audience
Government agencies and GLCs judging whether WCH is credible; Sarawak contractors and suppliers looking for work packages; partners and customers looking for hosting capacity.

## Job of the site
Prove credibility through specific, sourced facts and named people, then route each audience to contact.

## Constraints
- Bilingual: English at `/`, Bahasa Melayu at `/ms/`. Every page in both.
- Never mention crypto, mining, bitcoin, blockchain, payback or revenue figures.
- Every market statistic carries source and date; project figures are labelled management-provided.
- No invented clients, partners, certifications, addresses or dates. Empty data stays hidden.
- Photos of Bakun, land and the data hall are representative, not the WCH site, and are credited as such.
- WCAG AA contrast, no horizontal scroll at 375px.

## Brand commitments
Navy `#0B1F33` stays as the brand ink.
